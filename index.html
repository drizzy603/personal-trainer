How It Works

A progressive training programme with an AI coach — built as a single file, zero accounts required.

01

Four Screens

The app has four tabs, each serving a distinct purpose in the training workflow.

🏋️

Log

Today's session — exercises, sets, reps, weight and RPE fields ready to log. Empty until you build a programme via the AI Coach. Run and sport sub-tabs work independently.

📈

Progress

Charts and PRs across all exercises. See your strength curve, run pace, and bodyweight over the full programme.

💬

Coach

Claude-powered chat that reads your actual training data — and builds, edits, or regenerates your programme directly from conversation.

⚙️

Settings

API key, themes, programme overview, export/import backup, and this walkthrough.

02

Your Programme

There's no built-in plan. The first time you open the app the Log tab is empty — the AI Coach builds your programme during a short intake conversation, then it appears in Log automatically.

1

Start intake

Tap Build my programme from the empty Log tab or from the Coach tab. The Coach asks ten questions covering goals, frequency, split, equipment, injuries, current strength, experience, running, preferences, and programme length (4–24 weeks).

2

Programme generated

The Coach builds every week of the programme in a single tool call. You see live progress as it commits — exercises, weights, RPE targets, and your personal weekly schedule.

3

Train

The Log tab now shows today's session. Run days, rest days, and the cadence are all driven by the schedule the AI built for you.

Programmes are built around training blocks — common shapes the AI follows:

Base

Foundation

Moderate loads, technique focus, establish consistency.

Hypertrophy

Volume

Volume + load, muscle building, RPE climbs toward 8.

Strength / Peak

Intensity

Heavier loads, lower reps, peaking toward PR attempts.

Deload

Recovery

Light loads, full range of motion. Let the work sink in.

03

Logging a Session

Tap through each exercise on the Log tab. When you're done, the session saves to your history.

1

Open Log tab

The default session is whatever your weekly cadence has scheduled for today — Push, Pull, or Legs. You can tap the session pills to switch.

2

Log each set

Enter weight and reps for each set. RPE is optional — leave it unrated and history shows — instead of a fake number.

3

PRs detected automatically

If you lift more than your previous best, the app flags it as a PR in real time.

4

Save session

Tap Save session. The session is saved to localStorage. When you've completed all three lift sessions for the week, you're prompted to advance to the next week.

5

Log your run or sport

Switch to the Run sub-tab to log distance/time/HR, or the Sport sub-tab for cycling, pickleball, swimming, etc.

04

Data Storage

Everything lives in your browser — no server, no account, no sync. Export a JSON backup any time from Settings.

kt_routine
Your AI-generated programme — name, weeks (push/pull/legs exercises and any runs/sports planning per week), and weekPlan (Mon→Sun cadence)
kt_routine_backup
Snapshot of the previous routine before the Coach last edited it — powers the Restore Previous button
kt_sessions
All logged gym sessions with exercises, sets, reps, weights and RPE
kt_runs
Run logs — distance, time, heart rate, type, date
kt_sports
Sport activity logs (cycling, pickleball, swimming, etc.)
kt_weights
Working weights map — exercise name → current target weight in lb
kt_prs
Personal records — exercise name → max weight ever lifted
kt_week
Current week number (1 through your programme length)
kt_bw
Array of bodyweight entries — {date, weight} — most recent first
kt_run_goal
Target pace per km as "mm:ss" (e.g. "6:00")
kt_5k_goal
Target 5k finish time in seconds
kt_bench_goal / kt_squat_goal / kt_bw_goal
Lift and bodyweight targets in lb (0 until you set one)
kt_apikey
Your Anthropic API key — stored only on this device, sent only to Claude
kt_theme
Selected theme (dark / light / ocean / sunset / auto)
kt_coach_msgs
Coach chat history — persists across sessions, last 60 messages kept

Backup tip

Use the Export button in Settings to download a JSON file. If you ever clear your browser data or switch devices, import the file to restore everything.

05

Progress Tab

The Progress screen turns your logged data into charts — no setup required.

📊

Strength Charts

Weight over time for each exercise. See your progression week by week.

🏆

PR Board

Your all-time best weight for every exercise, updated automatically when you hit a new high.

🏃

Run Stats

Pace, time and heart rate trends across all logged runs.

⚖️

Bodyweight

Track your weight over the programme with a simple line chart.

06

AI Coach

The Coach tab connects to Claude via your Anthropic API key. Every message automatically includes your real training data — so advice is always about your actual sessions, not generic tips. Conversation history persists across sessions.

1

Coach home menu

Tapping the Coach tab shows a landing menu — open the chat, view conversation count, or disconnect. You're never dropped straight into the last message.

2

App builds context automatically

Your session history, PRs, current week, weekly cadence, run schedule, exercise weights, and goals are injected into the system prompt on every message.

3

Claude streams a response

The reply is specific to your numbers, streamed word-by-word in real time.

4

Coach can update the app directly

Ask the coach to change something and it will update the app without you leaving the chat. Supported actions:

  • Build my programme — runs the 10-question intake
  • Edit one week — "make week 5 a deload"
  • Change cadence — "swap Wed for a Pull day, drop Sunday's run"
  • Rewrite the programme — "regenerate from this week onward"
  • Exercise weights — "bump my bench to 172.5 lb"
  • Current week — "move me to week 4"
  • Log bodyweight — "I'm 181 lb today"
  • Goals — "set my pace goal to 6:00/km", "5k target 30:00", "bench goal 200"

How coach actions work

The coach uses native tool calls — each change is a structured, validated action the app executes immediately. You see a small green pill under the reply confirming what was updated. Past weeks are locked so completed training never gets rewritten, and the previous routine is backed up before any rewrite so you can Restore Previous if you don't like the new one.

07

Delivery & PWA

The app is a single index.html file deployed via a GitHub push. No build step, no framework, no dependencies.

📄

Single File

All HTML, CSS and JavaScript in one file. Open it directly or serve it from anywhere.

⚡

Instant Deploy

Push to GitHub → Pages auto-deploys in seconds. No CI, no config.

📱

Add to Home Screen

Works as a PWA. Add it to your iPhone home screen and it opens full-screen like a native app.

🔒

Offline Ready

Once loaded, it works without internet. Data stays on-device.

08

Render Loop

The app uses a minimal reactive pattern — no framework required.

1

State lives in localStorage

Sessions, runs, PRs and week number are read from localStorage on every render.

2

User action triggers a save

Any input — logging a set, completing a session — writes to localStorage immediately.

3

render() redraws the screen

The active tab re-renders from scratch using the latest state. Fast, simple, no virtual DOM.

4

Streamed responses

Coach replies appear word by word in real time. The conversation is saved so you can pick up where you left off.

API key required

The AI Coach needs an Anthropic API key to work. Add yours in Settings — it's stored only on your device and never sent anywhere except directly to Claude.

Privacy Policy

Trovo · Last updated April 29, 2026

The short version

Trovo stores all your data on your own device. We don't collect it, we don't sell it, and we don't have access to it. Your training data is yours.

01

Who We Are

Trovo is operated by Trovo ("we", "us", or "our"). If you have any questions about this policy, you can contact us at robertokalanisosa@outlook.com.

02

What Data We Collect

Trovo is a local-first application. The following data is stored exclusively on your device using your browser's localStorage or the app's local storage — it never leaves your device or reaches our servers:

  • Workout sessions (exercises, sets, reps, weight, RPE)
  • Run and sport activity logs
  • Bodyweight entries
  • Personal records (PRs)
  • Your AI-generated training programme
  • AI Coach conversation history
  • App preferences (theme, goals)

We do not operate servers that receive, store, or process your personal training data.

03

Anthropic API Key

The AI Coach feature requires an Anthropic API key, which you supply yourself in the app's Settings. This key is stored only on your device and is used solely to send your messages directly to Anthropic's API.

We never see, store, or transmit your API key. Your conversations with the AI Coach are sent directly from your device to Anthropic and are subject to Anthropic's Privacy Policy.

04

Apple Health (iOS app only)

On the iOS app, you may optionally allow Trovo to read your running workouts from Apple Health to save you re-entering them. This is opt-in: you grant permission through iOS's standard Health permission prompt the first time you tap "Import from Apple Health" in Settings, and you can revoke it at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Trovo.

When you import, Trovo reads workout date, duration, distance, and average heart rate for runs only — directly from Apple Health on your device. The data is then stored alongside your other Trovo runs in local storage. It never leaves your device and is never sent to us or any third party. Trovo never writes data back to Apple Health.

05

Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, or any account information
  • Location data
  • Health or biometric data beyond what you manually enter or what you opt to import from Apple Health (which stays on your device)
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage analytics or behavioural tracking data
  • Crash reports or diagnostic data sent to our servers
06

Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party service:

  • Anthropic — powers the AI Coach when you use that feature with your own API key. Subject to Anthropic's Privacy Policy.

We do not use advertising networks, analytics platforms, or data brokers.

07

Data Storage and Security

All data is stored locally on your device. We have no access to it and therefore cannot recover it if lost. We recommend using the Export feature in Settings to regularly back up your data as a JSON file.

Clearing your browser data or uninstalling the app will permanently delete all locally stored data.

08

Children's Privacy

Trovo is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, please contact us and we will address it promptly.

09

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

10

Contact

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at robertokalanisosa@outlook.com.
