02

Your programme

No built-in plan. The Coach builds yours during a short intake — then it appears in Log automatically.

1

Start intake

The Coach asks ten questions covering goals, frequency, equipment, injuries, and current strength.

2

Programme generated

Every week built in a single tool call. Live progress as it commits.

3

Train

Log shows today. Run days, rest days, cadence — all driven by your schedule.

Programmes are built around training blocks — common shapes the AI follows:

Base
Foundation
Moderate loads, technique, consistency.
Build
Hypertrophy
Volume + load, RPE climbs toward 8.
Peak
Strength
Heavier, lower reps. PR attempts.
Deload
Recovery
Light loads. Let the work sink in.
03

In the gym

The runner coaches while you lift. Everything here is computed on your phone — it works offline, with no API key.

Warm-up ramp
The main lift opens with a bar-up protocol sized to your working weight.
BAR × 10 · 90 × 5 · 135 × 3 · 180 × 1
Plate math
Barbell lifts show per-side plates under the weight, updating as you change it.
45 + 25 + 2.5 / SIDE
Progression hints
Hit every rep at RPE 7 or under last time? A chip offers the next weight — tap it to apply.
TRY 190 LB → LAST 185 × 8 @ RPE 7
Rest timer
Starts itself after every set. Trim or extend it without leaving the card.
PR detection
Beat an all-time best and the session says so the moment you finish — and keeps it on record.

05

Tips & tricks

The Coach knows your full history — every session, run, weight, and goal. These prompts unlock the most useful things it can do.

Log first, then ask
Finish your session, then open Coach. It already sees your reps, weight, and RPE — no need to paste anything.
"how was that session?"
Send a screenshot
Garmin, Strava, Apple Watch summary, gym whiteboard — attach an image and the Coach reads it and logs the data for you.
[attach image] "log this run"
Get a pre-session brief
Ask before you train. The Coach tells you what's on the plan, what to prioritise, and what to watch out for.
"what's the plan today and what should I focus on?"
Report what failed
Missed a lift or had to cut a session short? Tell the Coach — it adjusts load or volume for the next session automatically.
"couldn't hit the 3rd set on bench at 185, shoulder was pulling"
Log activities by chat
Tell it about a run, ride, or sport session in plain English and it saves it to your history — no need to open the Log tab.
"I ran 8km in 45 minutes this morning, easy pace"
Fix a wrong entry
Wrong weight, wrong date, a duplicate — the Coach can delete or correct any log directly without you digging through the calendar.
"delete that last session, I entered the wrong weights"
End-of-block check-in
At the end of a hard block, tell it how you feel. It rewrites the next block's load and recovery before you start it.
"week 8 was brutal — adjust week 9 to be a bit lighter"
Undo any programme change
If a Coach edit isn't right, roll it back instantly — no questions asked.
Settings → Data → Restore Previous Programme
Peek any lift's trend
Tap an exercise in today's session list to see its last eight logged weights as a sparkline — before you decide how heavy to go.
165 → 190 LB · 8 SESSIONS
Back up now and then
Your data lives only on this phone — there is no server copy. Export downloads a file you can restore anytime, on any device.
Settings → Data → Export session log
06

On your iPhone

The native app goes beyond the gym — widgets, alerts, and Apple Health working in the background.

Home-screen widget
Today's session, week progress, and streak at a glance — rolls over at midnight and flips to done when you finish.
Rest timer on your Lock Screen
The countdown runs as a Live Activity in the Dynamic Island — no need to keep the app open between sets.
Apple Health, both directions
Watch workouts — runs, rides, swims, pickleball, and more — land in your log with one tap, or automatically. Turn on saving and finished lifts count toward your rings.
Settings → Data → Apple Health
Background sync
With background sync on, iOS wakes Supero when a workout ends — the widget updates before you even open the app.
Training-day reminders
An optional 9:00 nudge on training days. Rest-timer alerts fire even with the phone locked.
Daily auto-backup
A backup file is written to the Files app every day — and to iCloud Drive when available, so your training history survives a lost phone.
Files → iCloud Drive → Supero

SUPERO · LAST UPDATED 6 JUL 2026

The short version
  • No accounts.
  • No tracking.
  • No analytics.
  • No ads.

01

On-device storage

Every workout, run, weigh-in, and chat lives in localStorage on your phone. We do not have a server — there is nothing to leak. Clearing browser data or uninstalling the app deletes everything; export a JSON backup from Settings to keep a copy.

02

No accounts

No email. No password. No login. The app does not even support accounts. There is nothing to recover and nothing to be hacked.

03

Anthropic API key

Your key is stored only on this device. Coach messages go directly from your phone to Anthropic — we never see, store, or transmit them. Conversations are subject to Anthropic's Privacy Policy.

04

Apple Health

Opt-in. If you grant permission, Supero reads your workouts only — runs, rides, and sports like swimming, tennis, or pickleball; date, distance, duration, average HR — and stores them locally alongside your other data. Background Watch sync (optional) lets iOS wake Supero briefly so new workouts are ready when you open the app; everything stays on-device. Imported workouts count as training data like any other, so the Coach includes recent ones in what it sends to Anthropic when you chat. Writing goes the other way only if you turn on "Save lifts to Apple Health" — then finished lifting sessions are added to Health as strength workouts; otherwise Supero never writes to Health. Revoke any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Supero.

05

Analytics

None. No tracking SDK, no advertising network, no data broker. The app does not phone home. Supero is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect any information from children.
