Privacy Policy

Supero · Last updated: July 6, 2026

The short version: Supero stores all your data on your own device. We don't collect it, we don't sell it, and we don't have access to it. Your training data is yours.

1. Who We Are

Supero is operated by Supero ("we", "us", or "our"). If you have any questions about this policy, you can contact us at robertokalanisosa@outlook.com.

2. What Data We Collect

Supero is a local-first application. The following data is stored exclusively on your device using your browser's localStorage or the app's local storage — none of it is ever sent to us, and we operate no servers that could receive it:

  • Workout sessions (exercises, sets, reps, weight, RPE)
  • Run and sport activity logs
  • Bodyweight entries
  • Personal records (PRs)
  • Your AI-generated training programme
  • AI Coach conversation history
  • App preferences (theme, goals)

We do not operate servers that receive, store, or process your personal training data.

If you use the AI Coach, the relevant parts of this data (your programme, recent training history, and your messages) are sent directly from your device to Anthropic to generate the Coach's responses — see Sections 3 and 6. If you never use the Coach, nothing leaves your device.

3. Anthropic API Key

The AI Coach feature requires an Anthropic API key, which you supply yourself in the app's Settings. This key is stored only on your device and is used solely to send your messages directly to Anthropic's API.

We never see, store, or transmit your API key. Your conversations with the AI Coach are sent directly from your device to Anthropic and are subject to Anthropic's Privacy Policy.

4. Apple Health (iOS app only)

On the iOS app, you may optionally allow Supero to read your running and cycling workouts from Apple Health to save you re-entering them. This is opt-in: you grant permission through iOS's standard Health permission prompt the first time you tap "Import from Apple Health" in Settings, and you can revoke it at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Supero.

When you import, Supero reads workout date, duration, distance, and average heart rate for runs and rides only — directly from Apple Health on your device. The data is then stored alongside your other Supero activity in local storage. It is never sent to us.

If you turn on "Background Watch sync" in Settings, iOS can wake Supero briefly when a new run or ride is saved to Apple Health so the workout is ready in your log the next time you open the app. This processing happens entirely on your device.

Separately, you may turn on "Save lifts to Apple Health" in Settings. When enabled, Supero writes the lifting sessions you finish in the app to Apple Health as strength-training workouts (start time, end time, and workout type only), so they count toward your activity rings. This is off by default, uses its own iOS Health permission prompt, and Supero only ever writes workouts you logged yourself — nothing else is written to Health.

Imported workouts become part of your training data, just like activity you log by hand. This means that if you use the AI Coach, your recent runs and rides — including imported ones and their average heart rate — are included in the context sent directly from your device to Anthropic, as described in Section 3. If you never use the Coach, imported Health data stays on your device.

5. Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, or any account information
  • Location data
  • Health or biometric data beyond what you manually enter or what you opt to import from Apple Health (stored on your device; shared with Anthropic only if you use the AI Coach)
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage analytics or behavioural tracking data
  • Crash reports or diagnostic data sent to our servers

6. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party service:

  • Anthropic — powers the AI Coach when you use that feature with your own API key. Subject to Anthropic's Privacy Policy.

We do not use advertising networks, analytics platforms, or data brokers.

7. Data Storage and Security

All data is stored locally on your device. We have no access to it and therefore cannot recover it if lost. We recommend using the Export feature in Settings to regularly back up your data as a JSON file.

Clearing your browser data or uninstalling the app will permanently delete all locally stored data.

8. Children's Privacy

Supero is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, please contact us and we will address it promptly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
